Days 1 to 30

Foundation.

We find where the money already is, then stop it leaking. Nothing new gets built until the account can hold what it already has.

The audit, on your own data

We pull your order export and your panel and compute your numbers: repeat rate, cohorts, the real gap between first and second order, capture funnel, flow estate, deliverability. No benchmarks, no templates.

Turn on capture

You are already paying for the traffic. A popup that asks the right question at the right moment turns those visits into an owned list, usually without a new discount.

Build the recovery layer

Browse, cart and checkout journeys on WhatsApp and email. The highest-yield flows in Indian D2C, and the most commonly half built.

Fix delivery before adding volume

Undelivered marketing messages are billed in full. We repair sender health and list quality first, so everything after it actually arrives.

The goal for month one
Automate the part of retention revenue that should never have depended on someone remembering to send something.

Days 31 to 60

Acceleration.

Now the calendar. We plan the full retention strategy and then send against it every single week, including holidays, launches and promotions.

A real calendar

Festivals, drops, launches and sale periods mapped for the whole quarter, so nothing is written the night before.

Every cohort gets its own send

Prospects, first-time buyers, two-time buyers, VIPs and the lapsed pool each get their own message and their own mechanic. One blast to everyone is how lists die.

Win-back, finally pointed at the lapsed

On most accounts we open, the largest untouched asset is the people who already bought once. They get a dedicated programme, not a leftover.

Copy in your voice

Guardrails agreed with you up front, then every send written to them. You approve fast because nothing arrives that you would have to rewrite.

The goal for month two
Campaign to every segment and measure. This is how we learn what your audience actually responds to, instead of guessing.

Days 61 to 90

Optimisation.

Every month from here we work to beat the month before it, on both flows and campaigns. You do not have to lift a finger for that to happen.

Winners become flows

Whatever performed as a campaign gets automated, timed to your measured rebuy window rather than a category guess.

Activation and replenishment, product by product

Post-purchase education specific to what they bought, then a reorder nudge timed to when that product actually runs out.

Split tests where the money is

We test the two flows carrying most of the revenue, not the twelve that carry none of it.

Reporting a founder can read

Two minutes, leading with retention's share of total store revenue. Not opens. It is the only number that sits next to your P&L.

The goal from here on
Beat last month. Every month. On campaigns and on flows.

So you know what you are buying

What we will not do.

  • 01 We will not build twenty flows in week one because a template says to. Campaigns are how we find out what works. Automation is how we keep it.
  • 02 We will not quote your channel mix before reading your data. Two Indian brands in the same year came out at WhatsApp 77% and SMS 54%. Anyone who tells you the split up front is selling you their template.
  • 03 We will not lead a report with open rates. Opens are inflated by tracking and they do not pay anyone.
  • 04 We will not reach for a discount first. On a list everyone had written off, relevance alone returned ₹2.2 lakh across two broadcasts at zero ad spend.
  • 05 We will not send anything you have not approved. Ever.
