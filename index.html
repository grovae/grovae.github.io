The problem

You are not sending too little. You are sending the same thing to everyone.

Every account we open looks different on the surface and identical underneath. The list is large, the calendar is full, and almost every message goes to almost everybody. Four things we find over and over.

  • 01
    The list is big, and quiet.One footwear account had 3.4 lakh subscribers. 1.5 lakh had signed up and never ordered. 50,000 bought once and disappeared for 200 days. All of them were getting the same newsletter.
  • 02
    Reach went up. Revenue did not.On one brand, a send to 81,210 people earned ₹69,035. A send to 37,627 people earned ₹4.27 lakh. Twice the list, a sixth of the money.
  • 03
    The discount quietly became the strategy.A segment goes cold, so it gets 20% off and that gets called retention. Do it for a year and the list simply waits for the next code.
  • 04
    Nobody computed the rebuy window.Four of five accounts we opened were running one replenishment flow on a 30 or 45 day delay. In acne skincare the second order lands on day 8, so that delay misses 82% of buyers.

The same offer, sent to people who had already bought twice, returned 9.4x. Sent to the cold list, 1.1x. Same creative, same discount. The only thing that changed was who received it.

Before you hire anyone

Four things we will tell you that a pitch deck will not.

Retention gets oversold, usually by people who benefit from the overselling. These are the caveats we would want if we were buying it.

  • 01
    It is not just a revenue channel.Judged on attributed rupees alone, you are seeing about a quarter of what it does. The rest lands as orders acquisition takes credit for.
  • 02
    It does not work in silos.WhatsApp alone, or email alone, leaves money on the table. That is why we will not run only one of them for you.
  • 03
    Half the outcome sits outside our control.Unit economics, product fit, landing pages, and how acquisition spends. Retention cannot fix arithmetic.
  • 04
    It is not a first-click channel.And usually not a last-click one either, which is exactly why it gets underfunded in most businesses.

So why pay for it. Because it is four channels wearing one name, and only one of them shows up in the revenue report.
